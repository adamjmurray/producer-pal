# Vision

Producer Pal exists to help people make music — not to make music instead of
them.

It's a creative assistant: a tool for exploring ideas, getting past technical
barriers, and turning musical intent into sound. The musician remains the
author.

**Mission:** bring any AI into Ableton Live — cloud or local, your choice — so
musicians can shape their music in plain language.

## Empowering musicians

Music production software is powerful, but it's hard to learn and slow to
operate. Producer Pal makes that power more accessible — helping beginners
express ideas before mastering every tool, and giving experienced producers
faster ways to work.

Accessibility also means physical accessibility.
[Voice control](/guide/chat-ui#voice-mode) lets musicians operate Live and make
detailed changes without depending entirely on a mouse and keyboard.

Accessibility doesn't mean removing depth or making decisions for you. It means
helping more people engage with that depth on their own terms.

## Freedom of choice

New technology should create freedom, not lock-in. Producer Pal works with
[many AI providers](/installation/choose-by-provider) — including
[local models](/installation/choose-local) — so you can pick what matches your
needs, budget, hardware, and privacy expectations. You can also skip AI entirely
and drive Live through the [REST API](/guide/rest-api).

There's no single "correct" way to use it. You control your models, your data,
your workflow, and [how the assistant behaves](/guide/customizing-skills).

## Open by design

Producer Pal is open source. Its values:

- **Openness** — free to use, study, modify, and build upon
- **Transparency** — no hidden behavior or misleading claims
- **Interoperability** — works across models, providers, and tools
- **User control** — your data, workflow, and creative decisions stay yours
- **Extensibility** — [adaptable](/extending) to workflows its creator never
  anticipated
- **Practical benefit** — judged by whether it genuinely helps, not by novelty

## Still evolving

Producer Pal started as one musician's experiment and is growing into something
for many. These principles don't prescribe how anyone should make music — they
guide how Producer Pal is built, and they're an invitation to help shape what it
becomes.
