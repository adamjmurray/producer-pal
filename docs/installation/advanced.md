# Other Options

Setups for less common cases.

## [Other MCP Clients](./other-mcp)

Already have an MCP-compatible app? Connect it directly with
[`npx producer-pal`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/producer-pal) — no dedicated
guide needed for your specific client.

## [Remote Access](./remote-access)

Reach Producer Pal from another computer on your network, or from claude.ai,
ChatGPT or Le Chat in your browser. Web chat is not recommended: it needs a
[web tunnel](./remote-access#web-tunnels), which puts Live on the public
internet.
