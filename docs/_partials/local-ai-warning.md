::: warning Needs a powerful computer

An Apple Silicon Mac with lots of memory, or a PC with a strong Nvidia graphics
card (8 GB of video memory at least, 16 GB or more is better). See
[Running Locally](/installation/choose-local) to check yours. On a regular
laptop, use [your AI subscription](/installation#option-1-use-your-subscription)
if you have one, or an [AI router](/installation/ai-routers).

:::
