The first time, the AI sets up Live for you:

1. It installs the [Producer Pal remote script](/guide/remote-script) and the
   Producer Pal device into your User Library.

3. Tell the AI when you're done. It adds the Producer Pal device to your Live

Don't want to restart Live yet? Drag Producer Pal from your User Library
(**Presets → MIDI Effects → Max MIDI Effect**) onto a MIDI track instead, and
